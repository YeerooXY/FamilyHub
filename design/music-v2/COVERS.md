# Album artwork sources

These are real catalog covers used in the design examples. They are not AI-generated. Catalog metadata was retrieved on 11 November 2026 through the Apple Search API. The selected record is retained in `catalog.json`; sample saved/readiness states do not describe current server inventory. Album matching in the actual player is still planned. A cover match never establishes that two source videos contain identical recordings.
