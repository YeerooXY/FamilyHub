Use case: logo-brand. Create a single compact text-free emblem for FamilyHub Music, a private music player with charcoal and soft lilac UI. A distinctive continuous rounded ribbon interlocking into a lowercase abstract h and a circular record groove, readable as one simple silhouette at 32 pixels. Flat solid pale lavender, very clean vector-like edges, centered with generous transparent padding. No text, letters written out, wordmarks, musical note symbol, gradients, shadows, mockup, border, arrows, labels, or clusters of three radiating strokes. Transparent background.
