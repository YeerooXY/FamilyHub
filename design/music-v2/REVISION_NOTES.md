## Local application changes

`music_web/static/app.js` reuses the existing queue, prepared jobs, two stable audio decks, transition endpoint and bounded preparation API. No backend service, dependency or new playback engine added.

- Failed next preparation excludes broken sources for the session, promotes the first ready remaining playlist recommendation, fills unique candidates and requests preparation in the background. With Autoplay off it does not select replacements. If nothing can play, an explicit empty state remains; there is no promise of uninterrupted playback without playable audio.
- Queue rendering and replenishment deduplicate source IDs. Pending transition requests reject stale settings; updated settings request a new plan immediately after the obsolete request settles.
- A playable incoming job can request a transition before job completion. Late seeking starts incoming audio at its cue and caps the overlap to the outgoing time remaining.
- Natural end/pause no longer cancels the incoming deck just before handoff. This was reproduced by the new browser test.

These changes are **local only**. The installed private app was not deployed or restarted. Tailscale, Paddock, tunnels and VM were untouched.
