          Storage and privacy

          Listen saves recordings to the shared server library. A single tick means audio is buffered on the server; a double tick means saved in the library. Search preloads visible songs (up to sixteen) of ten minutes or less into a bounded temporary cache. Upcoming tracks use the same cache. Search and new downloads contact YouTube. Artist names are sent to MusicBrainz for cached genre labels; unmatched artists remain Unsorted. Your phone and desktop stream from your private server.
